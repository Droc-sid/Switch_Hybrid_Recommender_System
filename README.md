# Switch_Hybrid_Recommender_System
To develop a predictive model that generates personalized product recommendations to enhance the customer experience and improve conversion rates.

 Justification and Significance
This research is significant as it provides e-commerce platforms with a framework to increase customer retention and reduce decision fatigue. Academically, it contributes to the study of "Switch Hybridization" logic—combining classical matrix factorization with deep learning to maximize predictive accuracy in sparse environments.

 Scope of the Study
The scope is limited to product recommendations within the e-commerce domain, specifically utilizing the online retail dataset. The study excludes music, movies, or real-time streaming recommendations.
